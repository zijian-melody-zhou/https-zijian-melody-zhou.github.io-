Vainglory Eternal Cup

Lead Organizer · Poster & Visual Design

Community esports tournament · 38 international teams

Online

Jul 2020

What I Did

  • Served as lead organizer — running registration (Jul 1–9), scheduling, and community operations through the finals on Jul 25–26.
  • Designed the tournament's official poster and visual identity, including regional editions.
  • Operated the official Discord community and tournament communications end to end.
  • Coordinated broadcasts in English, Japanese, and Chinese (Twitch, YouTube, and Huya).
  • Produced custom merchandise — self-decorated Vainglory merch for all top-8 teams, plus IGN rings and coins sold through the Discord.

Results & Impact

Billed as "The Final Tournament of Vainglory," the Eternal Cup brought together 38 international teams (CN, EA, EU, NA, SEA) competing for a community-funded prize pool that grew from US$1,500 to over US$1,600 by the finals, with trilingual broadcasts across three platforms.

Official poster & announcement — X · Match VOD — Twitch · Tournament Discord

Tournament merchandise I produced — custom Eternal Cup medallions (gold / silver / copper), dog tags, and keychains in branded gift boxes
