Cloudia

Sole Artist · UI Design & AI Art Direction

That Game Jam · two-week jam · team of three

itch.io · Windows

The game's UI is designed and made entirely by hand.

About

A cozy little game about gathering clouds and bringing life back to the world. As a cloud maker, you guide the wind — click and drag to make gusts — merge drifting clouds, and send rain to the regions that need it, watching each land bloom again. No failure, no time pressure — just breeze and rain.

My Role

  • Sole artist on a three-person team — all art direction and production, in two weeks.
  • Designed and hand-made the game's entire UI.
  • Trained and ran my own AI image models to produce the level environments, directing them into one consistent style across seven regions, each in withered and restored states.

Play on itch.io: shubingzhou.itch.io/cloudia

Level environments, withered → restored — about 70% AI-generated with models I trained and ran myself.

A Book of Magic

Producer · Main Designer · Main Artist

Indie puzzle game

University of California, Irvine

Fall 2025 – Present

Character sprites — five poses, drawn by me.

Content illustrations — all drawn by me, shown at one size.

My Role

  • Producer — planning and coordinating the project end to end: concept development, production schedules, quality requirements, and tracking milestones and deliverables against the timeline.
  • Main designer — designing the game's core tidying-and-sorting puzzle play: what each scene asks for, how its objects fit together, and how the one satisfying arrangement is found.
  • Main artist — creating all of the game's content illustrations: the spell-book shelf, dragon egg, potion shelf, gem box, and the pocket watch on the cover.
  • Playtesting and iterating on the design based on player feedback.

About

A cozy puzzle game about tidying up a magical world. Each level opens on a small, charming mess from a spellcaster's everyday life — spell books to shelve in order, potion bottles to line up, gems to sort back into their box, a dragon egg to settle onto its cushion — and asks you to put everything in its right place.

There is no timer and no way to fail. You look closely, nudge things around, and try a fit, until the arrangement clicks and the scene settles with a small, satisfying sense of order. Short, gentle levels built on observation — and on the quiet pleasure of making a magical household neat again.

About

A single-player, side-scrolling Roguelite of cute animals and bullet-hell combat. The protagonist descends into this world and, out of compassion for its creatures, gives up their own power to bring them strength and hope. Resurgence of Horrors

3D / 2D Artist · Concept Art

UC Irvine graduation project (ROH) · 3D top-down survival horror

University of California, Irvine

About

An ordinary person is caught in a paranormal event and learns of the Revenants — spirits from the other world that cause supernatural phenomena by fixed rules. They repeat their actions mechanically and attack anyone who meets their conditions; the player must stay calm, read each Revenant's pattern, and use everything at hand to escape.

The game is an exploration-driven puzzle chain through a haunted house and its grounds — an iron door and its key, a long candle, a red-lantern gate, a coded box, a coffin — where every solution hides in observation: diary pages, environmental clues, and the Revenants' own repeating behaviour. Thirty collectibles reward thorough players, including eleven diaries that piece together the house's story, hidden dialogues, items, and nine different death states. Built in Unity in third person, with an experimental first-person mode for screenshots and close-up scares.

Play on itch.io: iamayem.itch.io/resurgence-of-horrors

My Role

  • My largest project — a year-long graduation production with a team of 18, awarded first place of the school year at UC Irvine.
  • Concept art for the game's Revenants — the spirits that drive its horror — and for environment atmosphere and mood.
  • Produced 3D and 2D art assets for the game.
